Where your information goes

Plant names, diary notes and diary photos stay in this browser’s local storage. Bloom does not upload plant names, diary notes or photos to a server or sync them between devices. Edited watering reminder dates, times, intervals and plant identifiers are saved securely on the server and linked to this browser by a cookie. Clearing cookies loses access to those reminder settings. Other people using this browser profile may see them. Clearing browser data removes them, so keep a separate copy of important memories. Watering ringtone and browser notifications are optional and run while this page is open. Sound must be enabled again after a reload. Notification text can include your plant names and may appear on your device’s notification screen. Bloom includes no advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process normal request information to serve the site.

Photo previews and clue-based suggestions run in your browser. Photo contents are not analyzed for identification. Weather is optional: only after you press a weather button and allow access, your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo. Bloom rounds coordinates to two decimal places and does not save them. You can revoke location access in your browser settings.

Plant safety, pets and children

Never eat a plant or assume it is safe for pets or children based on Bloom’s suggestions. Confirm the species and toxicity with a qualified specialist or reliable local source. If ingestion or exposure is suspected, contact your local poison service, medical professional or veterinarian.
